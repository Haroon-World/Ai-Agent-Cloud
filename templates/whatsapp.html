💡 Why do temporary tokens expire & How to create a Permanent Token

The token on the Meta WhatsApp "API Setup" page is a 24-hour temporary token designed only for quick testing. When 24 hours pass, Meta invalidates it, and outgoing AI replies will fail.

To generate a Permanent Token that never expires, follow these 4 steps:

  1. Go to Meta Business Settings > Users > System Users.
  2. Click Add, name it ClinicConnect-Bot, and assign the role Admin.
  3. Click Assign Assets > Apps > select your WhatsApp App > toggle Full Control > Save.
  4. Click Generate New Token, select your App, set Token Expiration to Never, check whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management, and copy the token.

Paste that token into the form above, and your clinic will run permanently without needing manual refreshes!
